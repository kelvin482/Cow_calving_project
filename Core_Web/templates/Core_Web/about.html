About the system

Practical records for clearer farm follow-up.

CowCalving.farm is a digital farm-support platform for dairy farmers and veterinary professionals. It brings cow records, milk production entries, follow-up alerts, and communication into one connected workflow.

What guides the system

Useful records and timely follow-up should support a farmer’s decisions. They should not be presented as a veterinary diagnosis.

How the workflow works

From farm observation to informed follow-up

01

Keep cow records

Farmers register cows and record available identity, breeding, and calving information.

02

Track milk entries

Morning and evening amounts are recorded by cow and date, with a calculated daily total.

03

Review changes

Milk-drop alerts point to a change worth checking. Farmers can review the records and save observations for follow-up.

04

Connect with support

Farmer and veterinary workspaces support role-based access and communication with service providers.

Available now

What the system supports

  • Cow and breeding records
  • Morning/evening milk capture and daily totals
  • Rule-based milk-drop alerts and farmer observations
  • Farmer-to-provider messages and notifications
  • AI question-and-answer guidance

Clear boundaries

What it does not claim

  • A milk alert is not proof of illness or a diagnosis.
  • The AI assistant is not a veterinarian and does not replace professional assessment.
  • Milk forecasting is not currently an operational feature.
  • Alert performance has not been established on representative real-farm data.
