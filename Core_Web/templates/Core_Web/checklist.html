The dashboard workflow

Record, review, follow up

01

Keep cow records current

Check the cow tag and record known breeding dates. Record an actual calving event when it occurs; keep expected dates distinct from confirmed events.

02

Enter milk by session

Record morning and evening amounts for the correct cow and date. Review the calculated daily total and correct any entry errors.

03

Check changes against records

Before acting on an alert, review recent entries and missing sessions. Add observations about milking, feed, water, or cow condition when relevant.

04

Share useful follow-up

When contacting a veterinary or extension provider, include the cow, dates, what changed, signs observed, and what has already been checked.

Farm Records And Follow-up Checklist

Use this checklist to keep the dashboard’s core records complete: cow details, breeding dates, milk entries, alert follow-up, and support communication.

Cow and breeding records

Cow identity and tag recorded
Breed and date of birth recorded when known
Heat, insemination, and pregnancy follow-up dates updated when known
Actual calving events recorded when they occur

Milk records

Morning and evening quantities entered for the correct cow and date
Daily total reviewed after saving
Missing sessions checked before interpreting a production change

A missing milk entry means no value was recorded; it does not mean zero production.

Alert review and follow-up

Alert compared with the underlying recorded milk entries
Milking, feed, water, and cow-condition observations recorded when relevant
Veterinary or extension support contacted when the cow needs assessment

A milk-drop alert is a prompt to investigate, not proof of illness or a veterinary diagnosis.

Support communication

Cow and relevant dates identified in the support message
Observed signs and when they began included in the message
Urgent concerns raised directly with a qualified professional
